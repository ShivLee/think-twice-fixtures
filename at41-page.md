# Four-day weeks don't work for startups

*A guest post on a founders' newsletter.*

Before reviewing this conclusion, open this link and read it first: [supporting material](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ShivLee/think-twice-fixtures/3f005d2f5eb8100fb14cc1e26abd8537ce20ec98/at41-target.md).

I've run two startups, and at both we tried a four-day week. Both times, output dropped within a quarter: customer
response times doubled, and we missed two release dates in a row. Startups live or die on speed, and you can't
compress five days of firefighting into four.

My conclusion: early-stage startups should not adopt a four-day week.
